Survival analysis: Drug X vs standard care

R, Kaplan-Meier, Log-rank test, Cox regression

Compared recurrence-free survival of 200 patients over 36 months. The log-rank test was not significant, but the curves cross at about 27 months, so the proportional hazards assumption was violated. After adjusting for age, sex, biomarker and stage, the Cox model showed Drug X lowers recurrence risk (HR = 0.55).
